WHAT INSTITUTIONS REPORT

How to interpret positioning categories
CFTC DATA GUIDE
Managed Money
Funds and money managers; useful for tracking speculative futures positioning.
Producer / Merchant
Commercial participants such as producers, merchants, processors and users.
Swap Dealers
Dealers using futures to hedge swap exposures; not a simple directional bet.
Asset Managers / Leveraged Funds
Financial futures categories reported in the CFTC Traders in Financial Futures report.
CFTC reports aggregate categories, not named banks or each fund's live positions. Reports are generally based on Tuesday positions and published Friday.

SOURCE NOTES
BTC / ETH derivatives
Bybit public futures endpoints. Open interest is aggregate contract exposure; long/short ratio is account ratio, not exact institutional positioning.
GOLD futures positioning
CFTC Disaggregated COT data for COMEX Gold, reported weekly.
SEC 13F filings
Quarterly disclosed U.S. equity holdings. Not a real-time source and generally not BTC/ETH spot ownership.
